The brief

Fifty years of masala.
One new voice.

Sunder began in 1975 with masala made at home and sold from a bicycle in Indore. Three generations on, the brand needed to speak to a new kitchen without losing the elders who built it. The work: a brand strategy, a family of characters to carry it, the content, and a website that tells it.

The idea

Peedhi
badalti hai.Swaad nahi.

Generations change. Taste doesn't.

The brand's strength was never novelty. It was restraint, the elders' rule for the spice box: add less, keep the body happy.

So the strategy keeps the recipe and changes the storyteller. A family the reader already knows hands the brand down, one pinch at a time: ek chutki, full fire.
