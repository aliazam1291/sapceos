01 — Overview

A portfolio is a place,
not a page.

Most portfolios are a grid of cards. This one is a flight: you board at a galaxy, a ship flies beside you as you scroll, and each section is a place with its own object. Projects arrive as holograms, case studies are carried in by drones, and the tour ends with the ship landing on a pad.
